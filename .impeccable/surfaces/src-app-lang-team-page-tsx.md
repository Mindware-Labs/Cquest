# Team surface brief

- Scope and mode: `/[lang]/team`, Persuade.
- Audience and job: decision-makers evaluating Center Quest must understand how the operation is organized without reading employee profiles.
- Action: scan the six departments and their responsibilities, then discuss an operation through the quote flow.
- Content: the six client-supplied department names and four responsibilities per department; no employee photos, names, biographies or invented headcounts.
- Constraints: bilingual EN/ES, established Center Quest palette and Josefin Sans, WCAG 2.2 AA, responsive from mobile upward.
- Direction: a connected operating map in the first viewport resolves into one continuous six-cell departmental matrix below.
- Memorable moment: the same six specialties appear first as a coordinated network and then as concrete responsibilities.
- Unresolved: none for this release.
